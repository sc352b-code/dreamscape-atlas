Use `family-home-octopus-source-crop.webp` as the only image target.

## Preserve
- Preserve the crop composition exactly.
- Preserve all existing architecture, vegetation, water, paths, colour, lighting, perspective and texture.
- Preserve the painterly twilight language of Hearthlands.

## Octopus treatment
- Small, subtle and discoverable rather than dominant.
- Partially submerged in the water, with some body/tentacle form softened by reflection and depth.

- No glow outline, badge treatment, sticker edge or isolated cut-out appearance.
- Recognisable at close semantic zoom, but not attention-seeking at territory scale.

## Forbidden

- No labels.
- No UI.
- No panels.
- No borders.
- No counters.
- No icons.
- No tarot card.
- No additional symbols.
- No changes outside the minimum area required to integrate the octopus naturally.

## Output
Return the edited crop only, at the same 334×226 dimensions and same framing. The result will be composited back over the untouched 1672×941 Hearthlands master as `family-home-octopus-tile.webp`.

## Acceptance test
1. The octopus belongs physically in the landscape.
2. It shares the scene's light, colour and perspective.
3. It does not resemble a sticker or icon.
